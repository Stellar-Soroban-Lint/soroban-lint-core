# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that supports this community include clear, constructive
technical discussion, accepting good-faith corrections, and focusing feedback on
the work. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks,
discrimination, intimidation, and publishing private information without consent.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project.
Report conduct that violates it privately to ojuotimi932 through
https://github.com/ojuotimi932. The maintainers will review reports and take
proportionate action, including warnings, removal of contributions, or exclusion
from project spaces.
